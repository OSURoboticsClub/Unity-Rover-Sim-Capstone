# Unity-Rover-Sim-Capstone

## Project Description and Value Proposition

#### What is this?
A physics based simulation designed for the DAM Robotics Rover Team to emulate controlling their rover on virtual competition terrain.
#### What does this solve?
Every year the OSU Rover Team participates in a competition against other rover teams to simulate tasks befitting a Mars rover. These tasks often involve driving the rover manually or autonomously from point A to point B. However, the team sometimes has issues with tipping the rover during competition, usually by driving over steep terrain. This matters because it costs the team points on the tasks at hand, especially during autonomous tasks where manual operation or adjustments are prohibited.
